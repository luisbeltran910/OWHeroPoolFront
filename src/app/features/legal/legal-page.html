About this project

OW Hero Pool is an independent, non-commercial fan project made by Luxar for the Overwatch community. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Blizzard Entertainment, Inc. Overwatch and all associated names, images, and data are trademarks and property of Blizzard Entertainment.

Where the data comes from

Hero win rate, pick rate, and ban rate statistics are pulled directly from Blizzard's own official Hero Statistics page and refreshed periodically. Hero portrait images are loaded directly from Blizzard's asset servers. The Counter Picker's "recommended picks" are a community-sourced matchup guide based on aggregated kill-trade data from third-party trackers — not Blizzard's own statistics — so treat it as a strategic starting point, not a guarantee.

Accuracy

Data is provided "as is." It may lag behind the current patch, and can be temporarily unavailable if Blizzard's stats page changes or goes down. Nothing on this site is guaranteed to be accurate or current.

Privacy

This site has no user accounts, and doesn't use cookies, analytics, or trackers of any kind — it doesn't collect or store any personal information. The only network requests it makes are to its own backend (to fetch hero stats) and directly to Blizzard's servers (to load hero portrait images); the latter reaches Blizzard the same way it would if you visited their site directly.

Contact

This is a small hobby project maintained by Luxar. There's no support team, no warranty, and no formal terms beyond what's written on this page.
